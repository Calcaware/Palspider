Palspider

No AI overviews. Just links weighted by people.

Pages rank by how many other pages link to them - not by what a model guesses you want to see. Seed a URL and the crawler discovers the rest.

Crawl - 50 concurrent workers, cross-domain, rate-limited per host. Content fingerprints prevent duplicates.

Rank - TF-IDF scoring with in-degree link weighting. Pages earn authority when others link to them.

Peers - Add peer nodes in Settings. Queries fan out across the mesh; results merge deduplicated by URL. Peer lists gossip automatically.
